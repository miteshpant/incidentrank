**Observed but non-blocking**: the real Stripe event's `cancel_at_period_end` field read `false` even with a scheduled cancellation (Stripe sets `cancel_at` instead, in this API version, for Portal-initiated cancellations) — documented in `defects.md`; does not affect entitlement correctness, only an admin-only display column.

The actual #100/#101 concurrent-claim behavior is covered by the automated test suite rather than two real simultaneous live purchases: `founding.test.ts`'s "the 10th of 10 concurrent reservations... succeeds and the 11th sees sold_out" and the deterministic same-user race test (two genuinely overlapping Postgres transactions, forced via explicit `BEGIN`/`COMMIT` ordering on separate connections — not relying on `Promise.all` timing luck, which was found not to reliably reproduce the race), plus `checkout.test.ts`'s route-level "exactly one of two concurrent Founding checkouts for the last slot succeeds; the loser sees sold-out" test — all passing.
